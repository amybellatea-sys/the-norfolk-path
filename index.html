The book

The Norfolk Path

“A story about finding home, rediscovering yourself, and following the path that calls you back.”

The Norfolk Path is Amy Téa’s memoir of resilience, belonging and the extraordinary pull of Norfolk Island. Through memories, change and new beginnings, Amy explores what it means to find home — and to keep following your dreams.

About the author

Amy Téa

Amy Téa is the author of The Norfolk Path, published by Licola Life Press. Her memoir was born from a deep connection to Norfolk Island and a lifelong journey of finding home, courage and purpose.

Writing and publishing have been part of Amy’s professional life for decades. She has worked as a publisher and editor, editing more than 50 books on government topics, including an edition that was signed by the Prime Minister. Her career has taken her across the legal and finance arena, church environments, hospitality and other sectors, including more than 15 years as a night supervisor at Woolworths.

That breadth of experience has given Amy a deep understanding of people, institutions, work and the many different paths a life can take. The Norfolk Path brings those experiences together with her enduring connection to Norfolk Island.
